MasterChef Recipe Manager

Support for your personal recipe collection on iPhone and iPad.

Contact support

Email mg3putt@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot can help; remove personal information before sending it.

Sync between devices

Use the same Apple Account with iCloud enabled on both devices. Open MasterChef on each device and check the iCloud sync status under Import & Backup. You can select Sync Now there. Offline changes remain on your device until it reconnects.

Import a recipe

Use the built-in browser for supported recipe websites, or import screenshots from Photos or Files using the + menu. You can also share a webpage or image to MasterChef through Apple's Share button. Review imported quantities and text before saving.

Back up your collection

In Import & Backup, choose Export complete backup and save the file somewhere you can find again. Restore adds missing recipes while keeping existing recipes and edits.
