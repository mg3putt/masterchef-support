MasterChef Recipe Manager — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026.

MasterChef Recipe Manager stores the recipes, photos, categories, ratings, and notes you choose to save on your device. It uses your private iCloud database to synchronize your recipe library between devices using the same Apple Account when iCloud is available. Apple operates iCloud under its own terms and privacy policy.

Screenshot text recognition runs on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Screenshots are not sent to an external AI service for recognition. Screenshots you keep with a recipe form part of your library and may synchronize through iCloud.

The app accesses photos and files that you select through Apple's system pickers. It reads image data from the clipboard when you use a paste action. Recent recipe activity timestamps synchronize through your private iCloud database to keep Recents consistent across your devices. Session pins stay on the device.

When you use the built-in browser or import a website recipe, your device connects to that website and may download recipe text and images. Websites have their own privacy practices and may use cookies or other website technologies. The app does not operate those websites.

The app includes no developer-operated analytics service, advertising SDK, or advertising identifier tracking. We do not operate a separate server for your recipe library.

When you choose to share a recipe or export a backup, the information you select is provided to the destination you choose through Apple's sharing and file interfaces. A full backup includes recipe photos and original import records.

You can edit or delete recipes within the app. Recipe deletions synchronize through iCloud; minimal deletion markers are retained to prevent an older device from restoring a deleted recipe. Files or recipes you previously exported or shared must be managed separately at their destinations.

If you contact support, the information you send will be used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending private recipe backups unless needed to investigate an issue.
